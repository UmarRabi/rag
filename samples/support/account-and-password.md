# Account and password help

## Resetting your password

If you forgot your password, open the sign-in page and click **Forgot password**.
Enter the email address linked to your account and we will send you a reset link.
The link expires after 30 minutes, so use it straight away.

If the email does not arrive within five minutes, check your spam or junk folder.
Corporate email filters sometimes hold our messages; ask your IT team to allow
emails from no-reply@acme-support.example.

## Changing your password

When you are signed in, go to **Settings > Security > Change password**.
Your new password must be at least 12 characters long and include a number and a symbol.
For your safety, every other device is signed out after a password change.

## Two-factor authentication

We strongly recommend turning on two-factor authentication (2FA).
Go to **Settings > Security > Two-factor authentication** and scan the QR code
with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator.

If you lose your phone, use one of the ten backup codes you saved when you set up 2FA.
If you no longer have your backup codes, contact support with a photo ID to verify
your identity. Account recovery takes up to two business days.

## Locked accounts

After five failed sign-in attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes.
This protects you from people trying to guess your password. Wait 15 minutes
or reset your password to unlock it immediately.
