Shipping and delivery

We ship to every state in Nigeria and to most countries in West Africa. Orders placed before 2 pm on a business day leave our Lagos warehouse the same day.

Lagos and Abuja1 to 2 business daysSame day

Shipping costs

Standard delivery is free on orders over 50,000 naira. Below that, standard delivery costs 2,500 naira and express delivery costs 6,000 naira. International shipping is calculated at checkout based on weight.

Tracking your order

As soon as your parcel leaves the warehouse we email you a tracking number. You can follow the parcel in My orders or on the courier's website. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

Missed deliveries

If nobody is home, the courier tries again the next business day. After two failed attempts the parcel is held at the nearest pickup point for 7 days before it is returned to us.

Changing the delivery address

You can change the address until the order is packed. Go to My orders, choose the order and select Edit address. Once it has shipped, contact the courier directly.
